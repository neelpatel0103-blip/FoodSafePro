WHO WE ARE & OUR MISSION

About Us

FoodSafe Pro helps commercial kitchens replace scattered paper checks with a clear digital workflow for temperatures, cleaning, and corrective follow-up.

Staff can record routine checks, report issues, and keep related actions connected so managers can review what happened and what still needs attention.

“Our goal is to make everyday food-safety routines easier to follow and easier to review.”
